    Questions hosts ask us

    Is there a free trial?

    No. Bella is a paid subscription. You can cancel anytime, and your access continues until the end of the period you paid for.

    When am I charged?

    When you subscribe, and then every month or every year based on the plan you chose.

    Can I cancel anytime?

    Yes. Cancel from your billing portal or message us on WhatsApp. Access continues until the end of the period you paid for.

    Does Bella take a commission?

    No. You only pay your subscription and keep 100% of every booking.

    How many listings are included?

    Solo covers 1 listing, Growth up to 3 and Pro up to 5. For 6 or more listings, message us on WhatsApp or email us for a custom plan.

    Which languages does Bella speak?

    50+ languages, including Hindi, Hinglish, English, Arabic, French, German, Spanish and Japanese.

    How do I set Bella up?

    Right after payment, a one-step setup guide is emailed to the address you registered with. It takes about 60 seconds. There is no new app to install and no new account to create, because Bella works with your existing Airbnb account.

    Can Bella talk to Airbnb support for me?

    Yes. For emergencies, cancellations and urgent guest issues, Bella replies fast and contacts Airbnb customer support on your behalf, so a missed response is less likely to lead to a penalty.

    Can Bella file Airbnb claims for me?

    Yes, on any yearly plan, for up to 5 listings. Bella prepares and follows up damage or reimbursement claims with Airbnb for you. Airbnb makes the final decision on every claim.

    Is my data safe?

    Bella only accesses what you share through the co-host invite. Payments are handled by Razorpay and Stripe, and we never store your card details.
