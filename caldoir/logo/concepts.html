Five wordmark directions for Caldoir, in the tradition of the great houses, with no symbol. All lettering is outlined, so every file below is ready for a printer or engraver.

Wordmarks

Each mark in brass on Midnight Smoke, and in black on Bone White.

Roman inscription

Recommended

Carved capitals with flared strokes and wide, stately spacing, like lettering cut into marble. Fits Smoke on Stone and the engraved resin block.

Files: caldoir-inscription-brass.svg, caldoir-inscription-black.svg

Couture didone

High contrast between hairlines and heavy stems, in the Parisian couture tradition. Matches the Bodoni headline font in your notes.

Files: caldoir-couture-brass.svg, caldoir-couture-black.svg

Bold geometric

Heavy geometric capitals, tightly set, with a round O and a pointed A. The most modern and the loudest of the five.

Files: caldoir-geometric-brass.svg, caldoir-geometric-black.svg

Old-world serif

A sturdy Garamond-style serif with tight spacing. Feels like an old leather and saddlery house.

Files: caldoir-heritage-brass.svg, caldoir-heritage-black.svg

Plaque

A slim serif set on a small enamel plate with an inset border, like the metal plate on a leather bag. Works best on packaging rather than on its own.

Files: caldoir-plaque-brass.svg, caldoir-plaque-silver.svg

Monogram
