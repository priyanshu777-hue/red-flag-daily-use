Some men inherit it. Others earn it.

Caldoir Elixir opens on tobacco leaf and dark vanilla, settles into cured leather and warm spice, and dries down slow and smoky. It is made at elixir strength, so a little lasts the evening.

It is poured once. Three hundred and thirty-three bottles, each set in a clear block and engraved with its own number. When the last number is taken, it is not made again.

311 bottles of 100 mL come first. A smaller edition follows for those already on the list.
