# Caldoir Elixir: Session Notes

Date: 9 October 2026

## 1. Decisions made

- **Brand name:** Caldoir (sold as "Caldoir Elixir"). The name sounds French, so do not claim Swiss origin. Say "Swiss-inspired" at most.
- **Product:** one masculine tobacco-vanilla elixir (high concentration, roughly 25–35% oil, to be confirmed with the perfumer). Notes in the brief: tobacco leaf, vanilla, cured leather, warm spice, dry smoky finish.
- **Edition:** 333 bottles worldwide, each numbered.
  - 311 × 100 mL at ₹33,333 (launches first)
  - 22 × 50 mL at ₹22,222 ("The 22", a surprise drop once there is hype)
- **Sales model:** 1 month of marketing, then pre-orders with a 10% booking amount (₹3,333 for 100 mL, ₹2,222 for 50 mL).
- **Hype plan:** 3–5 months of scarcity-led marketing in the style of temple.com (dark, minimal, "coming soon"). The founder wears the perfume on podcasts.
- **Image:** old-money man in a cowboy hat with a cigar, in an Alpine chalet. Theme: "Smoke on Stone".
- **Bottle:** a clear resin block with a pebble-shaped glass flask inside, a brass cap and an engraved number. The reference bottle looks like an existing designer's piece. Commission an original design inspired by it, and do not copy it exactly.

## 4. Manufacturing steps

1. Perfumer brief and 3–5 samples from 3 perfumers (weeks 1–2). Options: Kannauj, Grasse, Dubai.
2. Lock the scent and get the written formula, IFRA certificate and allergen list (weeks 3–4).
3. Bottle prototype: design, resin sample, drop and leak tests (weeks 3–8).
4. Maceration and stability (6–8 weeks). Chill and filter so the elixir stays clear.
5. Packaging production (4–6 weeks).
6. Filling, QC, engraving and numbering, wave by wave.
7. Pack, insure and dispatch.

## 5. Compliance checklist

- The contract manufacturer needs a valid cosmetics licence.
- Labels need MRP, net quantity, batch, manufacturer and allergen details as required.
- Trademark: check availability for "Caldoir" in Class 3, then register it.
- Register for GST, and get an IEC and LUT for exports.
- "Elixir" has no legal definition. Do not promise a specific oil percentage unless the perfumer confirms it in writing.
- Do not write "Made in Switzerland" or invent a founding year.
- Indian law restricts tobacco advertising, so keep cigar imagery subtle and take legal advice.
- Disclose paid and gifted promotions (ASCI rules).
- Alcohol-based perfume is a flammable liquid. Check international courier and country rules before selling overseas.

## 8. Podcast and delivery

- Wear it on every appearance, and keep the bottle visible but not centred.
- Say one line: "Tobacco and vanilla, as an elixir. 333 bottles. That's it."
- Offer sample vials to hosts and guests. Cut their reactions into Reels and Shorts.
- Delivery: double-boxed and insured with signature. Include a numbered card, a handwritten note and a 2 mL sample vial. Returns only for transit damage.

## 9. Risks

- Copying the reference bottle: legal and reputation damage. Commission an original design.
- Resin block cracks or is too heavy: prototype and test shipping early.
- Cash gap: produce in waves and use your own capital.
- Elixir oil cost: get 3 quotes per item and keep a 15% buffer.
- Tobacco advertising rules: use subtle visuals and take legal advice.
- International shipping limits: check courier and country rules first.
- Weak demand: build the waitlist first, and aim for about 1,000 people for 333 bottles.
