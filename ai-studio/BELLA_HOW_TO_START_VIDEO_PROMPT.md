# Bella: "How to start" video prompt

A 40-second vertical video (9:16) with 5 scenes of 8 seconds each. It works with Google Veo / Flow, Higgsfield, Kling or Runway.

**Before you start:** upload these two images as character references, so Bella looks the same in every scene. They're in the repo under `bella-assets/`:
- `pose-typing.webp` (Bella)
- `bella-chameleon.webp` (her chameleon)

---

## Master prompt (paste first, or put it at the top of every scene)

> Stylised 3D animated short in a warm, premium Pixar-like style, 9:16 vertical, soft cinematic lighting, shallow depth of field, smooth camera moves.

## Scene 1 (0–8 s): the problem
**Prompt:** Night, 2:04 AM. A tired host sits on a sofa in a cosy Indian homestay, phone buzzing with message bubbles stacking up ("Where is the key?", "Wi-Fi password?", "Can I check in early?"). The host rubs their eyes. Bella walks in from the right side of the frame, smiles, and gently takes the phone. The bubbles calm down and turn gold.
**On-screen text:** "Guests write at 2 AM."
**Voiceover (Bella, warm female Indian-English voice):** "Hi, I'm Bella. Guests message all night. Let me take that off your plate."

## Scene 2 (8–16 s): step 1, choose your plan
**Prompt:** A bright cream studio. Three floating glass cards labelled "Solo", "Growth" and "Pro" rotate slowly around Bella. She taps the middle card, which glows gold and shows a big "1". A small padlock icon and a "Pay securely" button appear, and she taps it. A soft confetti sparkle follows.
**On-screen text:** "Step 1: Choose your plan"
**Voiceover:** "Step one: pick a plan, monthly or yearly, and pay securely. No commission on your bookings."

## Scene 3 (16–24 s): step 2, check your inbox
**Prompt:** Close-up of a phone screen floating in front of Bella. A new email slides in with the subject "Welcome to Bella: your 1-step setup guide" and a big "2" badge. Bella points at it and winks. The email opens to show a single red button: "Start setup".
**On-screen text:** "Step 2: Check your inbox"
**Voiceover:** "Step two: your setup guide lands in your email right after payment."

## Scene 4 (24–32 s): step 3, a 60-second setup
**Prompt:** Bella rides the green chameleon across a glowing timeline. A circular 60-second timer counts down beside a big "3". Bella taps "Start setup" and a single toggle labelled "Connect your bnb account" switches on. As it switches, the chameleon's skin ripples from green to gold. Text chips float past: "No new app", "No new sign-up", "Works with your bnb account".
**On-screen text:** "Step 3: One 60-second step"
**Voiceover:** "Step three: one 60-second step. No new app, no new sign-up. It works with your existing bnb account."

## Scene 5 (32–40 s): Bella goes live
**Prompt:** Morning sunrise over the homestay. The host wakes up refreshed to a calm phone showing glowing cards: "Replied in 3 s", "Check-in sent", "Weekend price +10%", "Calendar blocked". Bella stands confidently beside the chameleon and waves to the camera. End card: cream background, "Bella by Red Flag Homes" in an elegant serif, and a red button reading "Get Bella", with "redflaghomes.in/bella" underneath.
**On-screen text:** "Bella is live. 24/7, 50+ languages."
**Voiceover:** "That's it. I'm live: replying, pricing and checking guests in, 24/7. Get Bella at redflaghomes.in/bella."

## Settings
- **Format:** 9:16 vertical, 1080×1920, 24 fps. Make a 16:9 version for YouTube too.
- **Audio:** soft, modern lo-fi music with light piano and a gentle beat, kept quiet under the voiceover. Add a subtle chime on each step number.
- **Captions:** burn in subtitles for the voiceover (many people watch muted).
- **Negative prompt:** no logos, no real platform branding, no extra fingers, no warped text, no changes to Bella's outfit or hair, no dark or scary lighting.
